# TruNoise Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 30, 2026  
**App:** TruNoise (`com.trunoise.app`)  
**Publisher:** TruNoise  

This Privacy Policy describes how TruNoise (“we,” “us,” or “the app”) handles information when you use the TruNoise iOS application and its widgets.

If you have questions, contact us at: **support@trunoise.app**  
Support page: [https://trunoise.app/support](https://trunoise.app/support)  
This policy online: [https://trunoise.app/privacy](https://trunoise.app/privacy)

---

## 1. Summary

- We **do not** sell your personal data.
- We **do not** use third-party advertising or ad trackers.
- We **do not** use App Tracking Transparency / cross-app tracking (`NSPrivacyTracking` = false).
- TruNoise is designed to work **on your device**. Preferences, mixes, and playback state are stored locally.
- In-app purchases are processed by **Apple**, not by us.

---

## 2. Information we process

### 2.1 Information stored on your device

TruNoise may store the following **on your iPhone or iPad** (including via the App Group `group.com.trunoise.app` so Home Screen / Lock Screen widgets can show playback state):

- Playback preferences (for example last volume and noise color)
- Custom mix presets and sleep sequences you create
- Sleep timer state for display in the app and widgets
- A local cache of whether TruNoise Pro is unlocked (verified with Apple’s StoreKit entitlements)

This data stays on your device unless you back up your device with Apple (iCloud/computer backups are controlled by you and Apple).

### 2.2 Information we do not collect

TruNoise does **not** collect or upload:

- Name, email, or account profile to our servers (the app has no TruNoise user accounts)
- Precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or health data
- Advertising identifiers for tracking
- Analytics or crash reports to third-party SDKs (none are embedded for ads/analytics)

### 2.3 Purchases (Apple)

If you buy **TruNoise Pro** (non-consumable in-app purchase `com.trunoise.app.pro`), the transaction is handled by the **App Store / StoreKit**. Apple may process payment details and purchase history under [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We receive an entitlement signal that Pro is unlocked on your device; we do not receive your full payment card details.

Restore Purchases uses Apple’s purchase sync for your Apple ID.

### 2.4 Siri, Shortcuts, and system integrations

If you use Shortcuts, Siri, Control Center, or widgets, Apple’s system frameworks may process the intents and widget timelines you invoke. Those interactions are subject to Apple’s privacy practices and your device settings.

### 2.5 Required reason APIs

TruNoise accesses **UserDefaults** (including App Group defaults) to save preferences and widget state. This is declared in the app’s privacy manifest (`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`) with reason **CA92.1** (app functionality).

---

## 3. How we use information

On-device data is used only to:

- Play and mix noise
- Remember your settings and saved mixes
- Run the sleep timer and fade-out behavior
- Update widgets and respond to Shortcuts / deep links
- Unlock Pro features after a verified Apple purchase

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to data brokers.

---

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share on-device app content with third parties for marketing.

Limited disclosure may occur only if required by law, or as part of Apple’s normal App Store / payment systems when you choose to purchase Pro.

---

## 5. Children

TruNoise is a general-audience sleep utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Nursery-oriented sound presets (Pro) are ambient masking sounds only—not a children’s social or account-based product.

---

## 6. Data retention and deletion

Because preferences and mixes are stored **on your device**, you can remove them by:

- Deleting saved mixes/sequences inside the app, and/or
- Deleting the TruNoise app (removes local app data), and/or
- Resetting device content as provided by iOS

Purchase records remain with Apple and can be managed through your Apple ID / purchase history.

---

## 7. Security

We rely on iOS sandboxing, App Groups for widget state, and Apple’s StoreKit for purchases. No network account system is operated by TruNoise for login. You should keep your device locked and use a current iOS version.

---

## 8. International users

TruNoise is distributed via the Apple App Store. Processing of purchase data by Apple may occur in the United States or other regions according to Apple’s policies and your country/region settings.

---

## 9. Medical disclaimer

TruNoise is **not** a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Optional binaural tones are for relaxation only. If you have sleep, hearing, or health concerns, consult a qualified professional.
